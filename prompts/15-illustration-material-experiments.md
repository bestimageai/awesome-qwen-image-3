# Illustration and material experiments

Ten Qwen Image 3.0 prompt recipes. Replace bracketed inputs before generation. All quoted text is exact; no translation is implied unless explicitly requested. Outputs require human review.

<a id="ill-01"></a>

## ILL-01 — Layered-paper watershed

```text
Create a 3:4 educational paper-art concept showing a hill, stream and pond in stacked colored layers. Use clearly visible paper edges, restrained blue and green tones and soft shadows from above. Route the stream continuously downhill into the pond; leave the upper quarter for the supplied title. Render only [APPROVED TITLE AND LABELS]. No invented measurements or ecological claims. Variables: verified terrain relationships and exact copy; review scientific accuracy before teaching.
```

## ILL-02 — Two-ink neighborhood map

```text
Create a 4:3 illustrated map of a fictional neighborhood with a bakery, library, garden and repair shop arranged around one square. Use only cobalt and warm orange ink on ivory paper, with mild print-registration texture. Connect buildings with clear walkways and render only the four supplied place labels. No real addresses, logos, distance claims or inaccessible-route assumptions. Variables: fictional layout and labels. Keep ink overlap consistent and avoid introducing a third printed color.
```

## ILL-03 — Gouache botanical plate

```text
Use [VERIFIED PLANT REFERENCE] to create a 3:4 gouache study of one stem, two leaves and one flower on warm ivory paper. Preserve species-specific structure and use visible brush texture without obscuring anatomy. Place the supplied plant name at the bottom and no other text. Keep scale notation absent unless provided. No invented medicinal uses, decorative pseudo-Latin or altered petal count. Variables: reference, approved name and palette.
```

## ILL-04 — Neighborhood service illustration

```text
Create a 16:9 isometric illustration of a fictional shared courtyard connecting a library kiosk, tool shed, garden and seating area. Use four clearly separated zones, warm architectural colors and a continuous accessible-looking path only as specified in [APPROVED LAYOUT]. Render supplied zone names once each. No claims of code compliance, attendance or social impact. Keep stairs, doors and roof perspectives coherent. Variables: layout and labels; this is a concept illustration.
```

## ILL-05 — Embroidered icon study

```text
Create a square four-by-four sheet of sixteen embroidered-icon concepts from [APPROVED MOTIF LIST]. Use consistent cream fabric, navy and ochre thread, visible stitch direction and one centered motif per cell. Keep silhouettes simple enough to understand at small size. No text, duplicate motifs, copied logos or impossible floating stitches. Variables: sixteen motifs and thread colors. These are raster craft concepts, not machine embroidery files.
```

## ILL-06 — Small problem picture-book spread

```text
Create a 2:1 picture-book spread of an original young rabbit building a stepping-stone path across a shallow garden puddle for a snail. Use gentle colored-pencil texture, soft spring light and a clear left-to-right story movement. Keep the binding gutter free of faces and essential action. Leave the upper-left area blank for later typesetting. No words, extra characters or dangerous water. Variables: approved characters and palette.
```

## ILL-07 — Ceramic relief poster

```text
Create a 3:4 poster photographed as a handmade ceramic relief. Depict three stylized birds perched on curved branches, with cobalt glaze, ivory clay and small natural surface variations. Reserve a flat top band for "Clay and flight" and a lower band for "A studio exhibition". Render each phrase once, clearly embossed rather than printed on floating paper. No dates, sponsor marks or invented venue. Variables: approved copy and glaze colors.
```

## ILL-08 — Abstract editorial metaphor

```text
Create a square editorial illustration about organizing attention. Show one open drawer containing neatly separated colored paper shapes, while a single red ribbon trails outside. Use tactile collage, warm ivory background and soft shadows. Communicate order and interruption without words, human faces, company logos or medical imagery. Keep the drawer geometry plausible and avoid excessive visual clutter. Variables: approved theme and palette; the metaphor is a creative interpretation, not a factual claim.
```

<a id="ill-09"></a>

## ILL-09 — Linocut bread illustration

```text
Create a 3:4 original linocut-style illustration of one round loaf on a folded cloth. Use bold black carved marks, warm cream paper and a single terracotta accent behind the loaf. Keep the scoring pattern and crust volume legible, with directional hatching rather than random scratches. No text, bakery logo, signature or copied artist composition. Variables: bread shape and accent color. Preserve the visual logic of a limited-ink print.
```

## ILL-10 — Felt landscape miniature

```text
Create a 4:3 miniature landscape made from layered felt: two rounded hills, one small blue pond and three simple trees. Use visible fibers, stitched edges and soft studio light, with a playful but coherent scale. Keep exactly three trees and no additional objects. Add no text, faces, brand marks or plastic-looking surfaces. Variables: palette and approved landscape elements. This is a material-style illustration, not a photographed physical product.
```
