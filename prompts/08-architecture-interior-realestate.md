# Architecture, interiors, and property concepts

Ten Qwen Image 3.0 prompt recipes. Replace bracketed inputs before generation. All quoted text is exact; no translation is implied unless explicitly requested. Outputs require human review.

<a id="arc-01"></a>

## ARC-01 — Small reading alcove

```text
Create a 4:3 architectural interior image of a compact reading alcove beneath a staircase. Show one built-in oak bench, a shallow bookshelf and a round wall lamp, with a folded blue cushion. Use a believable human scale, straight verticals and soft daylight from a window outside the frame. Preserve usable headroom and clear access. No people, text, impossible cantilevers or oversized furniture. Variables: room dimensions, approved materials and palette; this is a design illustration, not construction documentation.
```

<a id="arc-02"></a>

## ARC-02 — Bakery counter renovation

```text
Use one authorized bakery interior photograph as the edit target. Change only the counter front to pale vertical timber slats and the wall behind it to muted blue. Preserve the counter dimensions, display glass, doors, ceiling fixtures, camera angle and all existing text. Match reflections and contact shadows to the original light. Do not add products, remove equipment or widen the room. Variables: source photo and approved surface finishes. Output one concept image without before-and-after labels.
```

<a id="arc-03"></a>

## ARC-03 — Furniture plan rendering

```text
Use [APPROVED FLOOR PLAN] to create a top-down 4:3 furniture-layout illustration. Preserve every wall, opening, stair and supplied dimension. Place only [APPROVED FURNITURE LIST] at the specified locations, using consistent scale and soft neutral shading. Keep circulation paths visible and render only supplied room names. Do not invent dimensions, move walls or imply code compliance. Variables: plan, furniture positions and labels; verify spatial accuracy separately.
```

<a id="arc-04"></a>

## ARC-04 — Blue-hour exterior edit

```text
Use an authorized property photograph as the edit target. Change only the time of day to early blue hour, with soft warm light visible through existing windows. Preserve the building, landscaping, neighboring structures, camera position and weather conditions unless explicitly specified. Keep sky brightness and window reflections physically plausible. Do not add lights, hide defects or alter views. No text or real-estate claims. Variables: source image and approved lighting treatment; label as a visualization when published.
```

## ARC-05 — Guest-room information card

```text
Create a 3:4 accommodation information card using [VERIFIED ROOM PLAN AND AMENITIES]. Put a simplified room illustration above a short list of supplied features. Preserve bed count, bathroom position and window placement. Use warm neutral colors, large type and only approved labels. Do not invent sea views, accessibility features, star ratings, dimensions or booking availability. Include "Illustrated room guide" beneath the heading. Variables: verified plan, feature list and approved title.
```

<a id="arc-06"></a>

## ARC-06 — Material palette board

```text
Create a 16:9 interior material board titled "Quiet Workshop". Arrange five distinct samples: pale oak, brushed aluminum, blue wool, warm plaster and speckled ceramic. Show realistic texture and thickness, with soft directional shadows and a clean cream background. Label each material exactly once beneath its sample. Keep sample sizes visually balanced without implying identical physical scale. No supplier brands, environmental certifications or performance claims. Variables: materials, labels and project title.
```

<a id="arc-07"></a>

## ARC-07 — Exhibition visitor flow

```text
Use [APPROVED EXHIBITION PLAN] to create a 16:9 axonometric illustration. Preserve all partition walls, entrances and supplied installations. Show a single approved visitor route with a blue line and directional arrows; keep emergency routes visually distinct only if provided. Label zones using [APPROVED ZONE NAMES]. Use neutral architectural shading and no decorative crowds. Do not invent safety exits, accessibility or occupancy figures. Variables: plan, route and labels; require venue review before use.
```

<a id="arc-08"></a>

## ARC-08 — Courtyard seasons

```text
Create a 16:9 four-panel landscape concept of the same small courtyard in spring, summer, autumn and winter. Lock the camera, stone path, bench, tree positions and building walls. Change only vegetation appearance, daylight and seasonal ground conditions appropriate to [VERIFIED CLIMATE]. Label each season once. Use realistic architectural visualization without moving objects between panels. Do not invent year-round flowering or mature growth rates. Variables: approved planting list and climate.
```

<a id="arc-09"></a>

## ARC-09 — Retail sightline study

```text
Create a 16:9 two-panel retail-layout concept from one approved floor plan. Show the same eye-level view from the entrance in both panels. The left uses the current shelf placement; the right uses only [APPROVED NEW POSITIONS]. Keep architecture, shelf dimensions, stock categories and lighting unchanged. Label panels "Current" and "Proposed" outside the images. Do not invent sales improvements, accessibility claims or extra floor area. Variables: plan and approved furniture moves.
```

## ARC-10 — Window-seat daylight study

```text
Create a 3:2 three-panel daylight study of one window seat at morning, noon and late afternoon. Use the same room, cushion, camera position and window orientation. Change only light direction and intensity according to [SUPPLIED ORIENTATION AND LOCATION]. Render "Morning", "Noon", and "Afternoon" below matching panels. Keep reflections and shadow directions consistent with the brief. Do not invent energy savings or measured daylight values. Variables: location, orientation and materials; this is illustrative, not a simulation.
```
