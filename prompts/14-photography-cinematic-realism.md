# Photography and cinematic realism

Ten Qwen Image 3.0 prompt recipes. Replace bracketed inputs before generation. All quoted text is exact; no translation is implied unless explicitly requested. Outputs require human review.

<a id="pho-01"></a>

## PHO-01 — Market morning editorial

```text
Create a 3:4 fictional documentary photograph of an adult florist arranging unbranded buckets of flowers before opening. Use an eye-level medium shot, overcast morning light and restrained natural color. Show believable hands, damp pavement and small imperfections in the stall. Keep background people indistinct and omit readable shop signs. No celebrity likeness, posed advertising smile, dramatic HDR or invented event claims. Variables: fictional subject, flowers and location character.
```

<a id="pho-02"></a>

## PHO-02 — Phone-camera breakfast

```text
Create a 3:4 casual phone-camera image of a half-finished breakfast: toast, a small dish of jam and a ceramic cup on a kitchen table. Use soft window light, moderate depth of field and slightly imperfect framing. Keep food portions realistic, crumbs varied and cup reflections plausible. No text, luxury branding, excessive background blur or polished studio props. Variables: meal, table surface and light direction; the image should feel everyday rather than a commercial catalog.
```

<a id="pho-03"></a>

## PHO-03 — Reusable product lighting

```text
Use an authorized product reference to create a square studio photograph on pale gray. Place one broad soft light at camera left and a subtle white fill at right; preserve product geometry, color, labels and surface wear. Keep one grounded contact shadow and natural highlight roll-off. No extra accessories, text, floating objects or over-sharpened edges. Variables: reference and approved background. Maintain this lighting description when generating related product images; identical output is not guaranteed.
```

## PHO-04 — Night bus shelter frame

```text
Create a 16:9 fictional cinematic still of an empty bus shelter at night after light rain. Use a warm overhead shelter lamp against a cool distant street, with modest reflections and deep but readable shadows. Show one folded newspaper on the bench without legible text. Keep perspective, glass reflections and light falloff believable. No people, route numbers, advertisements or real-location claims. Variables: shelter design, weather and color balance.
```

<a id="pho-05"></a>

## PHO-05 — Four-frame contact strip

```text
Create a 16:9 contact strip with four equal photographic frames of one original adult potter turning toward a window. Show distinct moments: looking down, beginning to turn, facing the light and smiling gently. Lock identity, clothing, room and exposure. Use subtle film grain and realistic motion differences, not repeated poses. No frame numbers, film-stock branding or text. Variables: fictional subject and studio setting; this is a composed still sheet, not extracted footage.
```

<a id="pho-06"></a>

## PHO-06 — Glass reflection study

```text
Create a square still-life photograph of one clear tumbler half-filled with water on a dark-blue table. Use a broad side light and a pale vertical reflection card, with a visible but subtle meniscus and believable refraction through the glass. Keep the rim elliptical, base grounded and water level horizontal. No text, ice, fruit, extra glasses or impossible internal reflections. Variables: glass shape, fill level and background tone.
```

<a id="pho-07"></a>

## PHO-07 — Food steam close-up

```text
Create a 4:3 close photograph of a plain ceramic bowl of vegetable soup beside a window. Use warm natural light and a thin irregular wisp of steam rising from the surface. Keep vegetables recognizable, liquid reflections coherent and the bowl rim clean. No text, utensils floating in the soup, excessive smoke, calorie labels or health claims. Variables: approved recipe, bowl and light direction. Avoid turning steam into a decorative solid ribbon.
```

<a id="pho-08"></a>

## PHO-08 — Quiet workshop wide shot

```text
Create a 16:9 wide interior photograph of a fictional repair workshop at closing time. Show a central wooden bench, one desk lamp switched on and neatly stored tools on the rear wall. Use a moderate wide-angle perspective with straight verticals, warm practical light and cool ambient fill. Keep aisles clear and objects physically supported. No people, readable brands, cinematic fog or impossible room geometry. Variables: workshop type and approved material palette.
```

<a id="pho-09"></a>

## PHO-09 — Shallow-focus material detail

```text
Create a 3:2 macro photograph of the seam between stitched canvas and worn leather on an unbranded bag. Focus sharply on three central stitches and let detail soften gradually toward the edges. Use broad natural light, realistic fibers and subdued colors. Keep stitch spacing, holes and material thickness plausible. No text, brand emblems, extreme sharpening or repeated texture stamps. Variables: authorized product detail and material colors.
```

<a id="pho-10"></a>

## PHO-10 — Long-exposure water concept

```text
Create a 16:9 fictional landscape photograph of a small stone footbridge over a stream. Use a long-exposure visual treatment that softens moving water while keeping bridge, rocks and foliage sharp. Place the bridge off center and retain believable water direction around rocks. Use overcast light and natural green tones. No text, people, recognizable landmark, excessive mist or glowing water. Variables: bridge design and season; do not claim an actual camera exposure or location.
```
