# Social posts, covers, and creator content

Ten Qwen Image 3.0 prompt recipes. Replace bracketed inputs before generation. All quoted text is exact; no translation is implied unless explicitly requested. Outputs require human review.

<a id="soc-01"></a>

## SOC-01 — Balcony growing tutorial cover

```text
Create a 3:4 social cover for a beginner balcony-growing guide. Show three small terracotta pots on a narrow shelf, each with a distinct leafy herb, and a compact watering can beneath. Use bright natural photography and a warm cream background. Render "A small balcony, a fresh start", "Three pots to begin", and "1/5" with the title occupying the upper third. No yield, health or guaranteed-growth claims. Keep pots grounded and railing geometry coherent. Variables: approved herbs and title.
```

## SOC-02 — Triptych craft announcement

```text
Create one 3:1 master image intended to be split into three square social posts. Show a long worktable with clay tools on the left, a ceramic pitcher in the center and folded linen on the right. Keep each square independently balanced; place "Make", "Share", and "Meet" in separate panels. Use consistent warm lighting and quiet cream margins. No essential object or letter may cross a crop boundary. This is a visual master, not three exported files. Variables: craft objects and three approved words.
```

## SOC-03 — Research finding card

```text
Create a 16:9 professional social card using [VERIFIED FINDING, DATA AND SOURCE]. Put one precise finding at the top, a simple supporting chart in the center and the source at the bottom. Use charcoal text and one teal accent on white. Preserve units, dates, sample limitations and uncertainty. Do not turn correlation into causation or add a dramatic percentage. Keep text readable on a phone. Variables: approved finding, dataset and citation; chart correctness needs human review.
```

## SOC-04 — Cooking comparison thumbnail

```text
Create a 16:9 video thumbnail comparing three ways of cutting a pear for serving: slices, cubes and a fan. Place three identical white plates in a row on a cobalt table, with matching pear color and consistent overhead light. Render only "One pear, three cuts" above them. Keep the cuts visually distinct and portions plausible. No nutrition claims, fake ratings, knives near hands or additional text. Variables: fruit and approved headline.
```

## SOC-05 — Desk reset short-video cover

```text
Create a 9:16 cover showing a half-tidy desk: papers aligned on the left, a closed notebook and cup on the right. Use natural overhead photography, soft shadows and a restrained sand-and-blue palette. Render "Reset your desk" in the central safe area and "Start with one surface" beneath. Keep important text away from the bottom quarter and right edge for platform overlays. No timer claim, app logos or extra copy. Variables: desk items and two-line title.
```

## SOC-06 — Two-guest live announcement

```text
Use two authorized adult portraits to create a 3:4 livestream announcement. Give each guest equal visual space, matching crop scale and neutral lighting; place names directly below their corresponding portraits. Render only [EVENT TITLE], [GUEST NAMES], [DATE] and [TIME ZONE]. Use a simple navy-and-cream grid without fake live indicators or platform logos. Preserve identities and do not invent credentials or attendance figures. Variables: two references and approved event copy.
```

## SOC-07 — Verified quotation card

```text
Create a square quotation card using [EXACT VERIFIED QUOTE] and [AUTHOR ATTRIBUTION]. Set the quote in large readable serif type on warm off-white paper; place attribution below, aligned to the same left edge. Add one small abstract blue line drawing in the lower corner. Preserve punctuation and wording exactly; do not shorten, fabricate or assign a quote to a different person. No portrait, signature or endorsement badge. Variables: verified quote, attribution and palette.
```

## SOC-08 — Nine-step craft grid

```text
Create a square three-by-three grid illustrating [NINE APPROVED CRAFT STEPS]. Use the same tabletop, hands and materials throughout, with clear progression from raw material to finished object. Label panels only "1" through "9", in left-to-right, top-to-bottom order. Keep tools and object proportions stable, and do not invent a step or show unsafe tool handling. No decorative words or duplicate panels. Variables: verified steps and materials; instructional accuracy requires review.
```

## SOC-09 — Event recap mosaic

```text
Create a 3:4 editorial recap layout for a fictional community drawing day. Arrange four image panels: sketchbooks on a table, hands drawing, a wall of unsigned sketches and empty stools at closing. Use consistent daylight and a small top heading "A day of drawing". Keep faces unidentifiable and artwork original. Do not add attendance numbers, sponsor marks or testimonial quotes. Leave a calm footer strip blank. Variables: activity and exact heading.
```

## SOC-10 — Vertical cover crop study

```text
Create a 9:16 creator-cover concept with a single red paper kite centered against a pale sky. Place "Build a paper kite" in the middle third, leaving the top fifteen percent and bottom twenty-five percent free of text. Keep the kite and headline legible within a central square crop as well. Use clean illustrative texture and a gently curved string. No platform UI, extra kites, fake buttons or small captions. Variables: object color and approved title.
```
