# E-commerce, products, and food

Ten Qwen Image 3.0 prompt recipes. Replace bracketed inputs before generation. All quoted text is exact; no translation is implied unless explicitly requested. Outputs require human review.

## COM-01 — Modular desk lamp hero

```text
Create a square catalog photograph of a fictional modular desk lamp. One matte butter-yellow lamp has a cylindrical weighted base, a single hinged arm and a shallow conical shade; place it on an ivory tabletop with a cobalt repair knob beside the base. Use soft light from the upper left and a gentle contact shadow. The only text is "LATCH" on the base and "Keep the light" in the upper-left margin. Preserve plausible joints and one power cord. No certification badges, prices or extra parts. Variables: finish and approved brand text.
```

## COM-02 — Lunchbox product views

```text
Using one authorized product photograph as the geometry reference, create a 16:9 presentation with four equal views of the same rectangular lunchbox: front closed, rear closed, open overhead and latch close-up. Use a pale seamless background and identical soft lighting. Label the views only "Front", "Back", "Inside", and "Latch". Preserve compartment count, seal color and latch placement in every view; invent no dimensions or material claims. Leave visible space between panels. Variables: product reference and translated labels.
```

## COM-03 — Three tea tins

```text
Create a 3:2 packaging concept for three fictional tea tins sharing one cylindrical shape. Arrange them in a straight line, with distinct cream, lavender and sage label bands. Use the shared brand "Field Notes" and the respective names "Morning", "Noon", and "Evening"; add no other text. Photograph on a neutral shelf with consistent highlights and scale. Keep lids, typography alignment and label width identical across all three tins. Do not claim ingredients, origin or health benefits. Variables: approved brand, names and colors.
```

## COM-05 — Bread making process

```text
Create a 16:9 four-panel image showing one fictional bakery's sourdough workflow: mixing dough, folding dough, scoring a shaped loaf and presenting the baked loaf. Use close documentary photography on the same wooden bench, left-to-right reading order and consistent flour-dusted hands. Label panels "Mix", "Fold", "Score", and "Bake". Keep exactly four panels and distinguish raw dough from baked crust. Do not add cooking temperatures or timing. This is an illustrative process concept, not food-safety instruction. Variables: dough type and approved labels.
```

## COM-06 — Lunch assortment

```text
Create a 16:9 overhead catalog image of four realistic lunch bowls in a two-by-two grid: roasted squash and rice, mushrooms and noodles, lentils and carrots, and tomatoes with couscous. Use identical shallow white bowls, distinct textures, soft daylight and a muted green table. Leave equal gaps and no overlapping utensils. Add no text, calorie counts, dietary guarantees or ingredient labels. Keep plausible portions and avoid repeated food textures. Variables: four supplied recipes, bowl finish and tabletop color.
```


## COM-09 — Refill package instructions

```text
Create a 3:4 packaging-use concept with three vertically stacked panels: empty bottle beside refill pouch, pouch nozzle pouring into bottle, and closed bottle next to folded empty pouch. Use the same unbranded amber bottle and ivory pouch in all panels, with clean technical illustration and sage arrows. Render only "Open", "Refill", and "Close". Keep exactly three steps, no liquid spills and no reversed pouring direction. Do not imply food or chemical compatibility. Variables: verified container design and approved instruction labels.
```

## COM-10 — Bundle contents flat lay

```text
Using [APPROVED CONTENTS LIST] and authorized product references, create a square flat lay showing exactly one of each included item in a starter stationery kit. Arrange the notebook centrally and smaller items around it with generous spacing; use warm paper texture and soft overhead light. Add only the title "Inside the kit" and the supplied item names aligned to their objects. Preserve item counts, relative size and colors. Do not add gifts, prices, warranty seals or accessories absent from the list.
```
