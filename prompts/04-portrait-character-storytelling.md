# Characters, portraits, and storyboards

Ten Qwen Image 3.0 prompt recipes. Replace bracketed inputs before generation. All quoted text is exact; no translation is implied unless explicitly requested. Outputs require human review.

<a id="art-01"></a>

## ART-01 — Mira and the book return

```text
Create a 16:9 four-panel story sheet about Mira, an original adult mobile librarian with short black curls, round glasses, a rust cardigan and a blue bicycle basket. In left-to-right panels she finds a book on a bench, checks its plain cover, places it in her basket, then delivers it to a small library doorway. Use warm gouache illustration and soft morning light. Keep her face, outfit, bicycle and yellow book identical throughout. Exactly four equal panels; no captions, speech bubbles or legible book titles. This is a still storyboard, not video.
```

## ART-02 — Natural portrait edit

```text
Use an authorized adult portrait as the identity reference. Create a 3:4 professional portrait at eye level, changing only the background to a softly lit pale-blue studio and the clothing to a plain charcoal jacket. Preserve age, facial proportions, hairstyle, skin texture and expression. Match the new background light to the face and keep shoulders anatomically natural. Add no text, jewelry, beauty retouching or altered body shape. Variables: background color and approved wardrobe; do not use an unauthorized person's likeness.
```

## ART-03 — Fictional station keeper

```text
Create a square portrait of an original adult station keeper in a small coastal railway office. The character has silver cropped hair, a weathered navy jacket and an attentive expression; a blurred wall clock sits behind them. Use natural film-like photography, overcast window light and visible skin texture. Keep the face central with space around the head. No resemblance to a named person, official uniform insignia, readable tickets or identity documents. Variables: age range, palette and fictional occupation.
```

## ART-04 — Seasonal courier sheet

```text
Create a 16:9 four-panel character sheet of one original adult parcel courier with a long braid, green satchel and red bicycle. Show spring drizzle, summer shade, autumn wind and winter frost in equal panels. Change only seasonal outerwear and weather; lock facial features, body proportions and bicycle geometry. Use clear editorial illustration, matching camera height and neutral poses. Render only "Spring", "Summer", "Autumn", and "Winter" beneath their panels. No brand logos or invented safety certifications. Variables: character design and seasonal palette.
```

## ART-05 — Otter picture-book spread

```text
Create a 2:1 picture-book spread showing a small otter helping a turtle retrieve a paper boat from reeds. Place the otter on the left and turtle on the right, with the boat near the lower center but away from the binding gutter. Use watercolor washes, warm afternoon light and readable expressions. Leave the upper-left sky blank for later typesetting. No generated words, dangerous water scene or extra characters. Keep animal scale and boat shape consistent. Variables: setting, palette and approved character references.
```

## ART-06 — Wordless umbrella comic

```text
Create a portrait 2:3 comic with six equal panels in two columns and three rows. An original adult opens a tiny umbrella, notices a wet dog, shares it, both get splashed by a puddle, discover a broad shop awning, then sit dry beneath it. Use clear ink-and-wash illustration with consistent clothing and one brown dog. Keep reading order left-to-right, top-to-bottom and actions causally connected. No dialogue, signs, extra panels or injury. Variables: character appearance and umbrella color.
```

## ART-07 — Tea advertisement storyboard

```text
Create a 16:9 six-panel still storyboard for a fictional twenty-second tea advertisement. Panels show a closed tea tin, hand opening it, leaves entering a cup, hot water pouring, steam near a window, and the cup beside the tin. Use one cream tin, one blue cup and consistent morning light. Render only the final-panel words "A quieter moment". Keep each panel a distinct shot and all props stable. No health benefits, brew temperatures or brand claims. Variables: prop design and approved final line; this is a concept, not generated footage.
```

## ART-08 — Historical street reconstruction

```text
Using [DATED EVIDENCE PACK], create a 16:9 illustration of ordinary activity at [PLACE] in [YEAR]. Preserve documented street alignment, building materials and clothing; show uncertain background areas with subdued detail. Include a narrow legend distinguishing "Documented" and "Interpretive" elements using supplied mappings. No invented inscriptions, famous faces, monuments or modern objects. Use restrained documentary illustration rather than presenting the scene as a genuine photograph. Variables: place, year, evidence and uncertainty map; obtain historical review.
```

<a id="art-09"></a>

## ART-09 — Expression reference sheet

```text
Create a 3:2 sheet of six head-and-shoulder views of one original adult character using [APPROVED CHARACTER REFERENCE]. Show neutral, curious, relieved, puzzled, determined and amused expressions in a two-by-three grid. Preserve skull shape, hair, age, outfit and camera distance; vary only eyebrows, mouth and subtle head inclination. Use soft studio illustration and a uniform pale background. Label each expression exactly once. No caricatured anatomy, identity drift or additional accessories. Variables: reference, palette and translated labels.
```

<a id="art-10"></a>

## ART-10 — Object continuity storyboard

```text
Create a 16:9 four-panel storyboard in which a red ceramic bowl moves from a shelf to a table, receives three oranges, then is carried to a window. Use the same bowl with one visible chip on its rim in every panel. Keep exactly three oranges after they appear, consistent room geometry and warm side light. Use clear realistic illustration, medium-to-close shot progression and no text. Do not move the chip, duplicate fruit or change the bowl's shape. Variables: object reference and approved room layout.
```
