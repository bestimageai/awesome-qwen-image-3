# Brand, posters, and campaigns

Ten Qwen Image 3.0 prompt recipes. Replace bracketed inputs before generation. All quoted text is exact; no translation is implied unless explicitly requested. Outputs require human review.

## MKT-01 — Repairable lamp launch

```text
Create a 3:4 launch poster for fictional studio LATCH and its repairable desk lamp. Show one folded ochre lamp on a blue workbench, with its removable shade beside it; the lamp occupies the lower two thirds. Use crisp product photography, soft side light and a restrained editorial grid. Render only "LATCH", "Made to stay", and "Replace a part. Keep the lamp." in descending hierarchy. Keep joints mechanically plausible and parts matched. Add no prices, sustainability certifications or performance claims. Variables: studio name, finish and approved slogan.
```

## MKT-02 — River reading festival

```text
Create a 3:4 illustrated poster for a fictional floating-library festival. A moored blue book barge sits at the lower right; a curved red footbridge guides the eye from the upper-left title to three readers under striped awnings. Use flat screen-print shapes, ivory paper and generous margins. Render exactly "河畔读书节", "RIVER READING DAYS", "川辺の読書会", and "SAT–SUN · 10:00–17:00", each once in a separate block. Preserve Chinese, English and Japanese as supplied. No other signs, sponsors or watermarks. Variables: approved festival copy and palette.
```

## MKT-03 — Sound archive podcast

```text
Create a square cover for a fictional podcast titled "Small Sounds". Show an enlarged brass key resting in a shallow tray, with three delicate concentric vibration rings drawn behind it. Use warm macro photography and a deep aubergine background; place the title across the upper quarter and "Stories worth hearing" below. Both lines must remain readable when viewed at 100 pixels wide. No microphones, headphones, platform badges or episode numbers. Keep one key, realistic reflections and clean negative space. Variables: title, object and background color.
```

## MKT-04 — Tool comparison thumbnail

```text
Create a 16:9 video thumbnail explaining hand-tool selection. Arrange three unbranded screwdrivers vertically on the right, with clearly different slot, cross and hex tips. On the left render only "Which tip fits?" over two lines. Add one small diagram of a matching screw head beneath each tip, without extra labels. Use bright tabletop photography, charcoal type and yellow background accents. Keep tips and head recesses mechanically consistent; no fake reactions, ratings or exaggerated claims. Variables: tool set and exact headline.
```

## MKT-05 — Packing tutorial carousel cover

```text
Create a 3:4 cover for a six-slide guide about packing fragile ceramics. Show a single bowl suspended visually between folded cardboard dividers, with a roll of paper cushioning at the bottom. Use an orderly overhead photograph on pale gray. Place "Pack with care" at the top, "A ceramic shipping guide" below, and "1/6" in the bottom-right corner. Keep the bowl intact and materials realistic. No shipping-company logos, guaranteed damage-prevention claims or additional words. Variables: object, title and slide count.
```

## MKT-06 — Community workshop recruitment

```text
Create a portrait 3:4 recruitment poster for a fictional bicycle-repair cooperative. Show an empty mechanic's apron hanging beside a neatly arranged tool board; leave the upper half clear for text. Use documentary photography, gentle window light and dark-green typography. Render "Join the workshop", "[ROLE]", "[CITY] · [SCHEDULE]", and "Apply by [DATE]" using supplied replacements. Keep tools recognizable and avoid invented salaries, certifications or contact details. Do not generate a QR code. Variables must all be replaced before generation.
```

## MKT-07 — Reusable cup reminder

```text
Create a 9:16 café notice encouraging customers to bring a reusable cup. Show one well-used cream travel cup beside a chalk-blue ceramic jug, photographed at counter height in warm morning light. Put "Your cup, your ritual" above and "Bring it next time" beneath the cup. Keep the middle spacious and lettering high contrast at viewing distance. No discount, environmental statistic or health claim; no additional objects or text. Preserve realistic lid geometry and grounded shadows. Variables: cup color and approved two-line message.
```

## MKT-08 — Lantern walk identity frame

```text
Create a 16:9 static key visual for a fictional evening lantern walk called "Paths of Light". A line of small amber paper lanterns curves through a dark botanical garden toward an open gate. Keep the center-left area quiet for the title; place "An evening together" immediately below. Use layered paper illustration with subtle depth and visible fiber texture, not neon effects. Avoid identifiable landmarks, sponsor logos and additional copy. This is one still concept frame, not an animated output. Variables: title, path shape and accent color.
```

## MKT-09 — Repair workshop poster

```text
Create a 3:4 poster for a fictional community repair workshop. Illustrate one terracotta table holding a yellow toaster, a small screwdriver and a spool of blue thread; two adult hands work on the toaster from opposite sides. Use clean cut-paper shapes, cream background, navy typography and generous spacing. At the top render "Repair Saturday"; beneath it render "Bring one small item"; at the bottom render "14:00–17:00". Render each line once and nothing else. Keep hands anatomically plausible and the toaster unplugged. Variables: approved localized text and palette.
```

## MKT-10 — Service opening window notice

```text
Create a 2:3 window notice for a fictional neighborhood photo-printing studio. Show three unbranded instant prints clipped to a single horizontal cord in the lower half. Above them render "Print your everyday", followed by "Opening [DATE]" and "[STREET]". Use tactile cream paper, cobalt type and natural shadows. Supply the date and address before generation; do not invent an opening offer. Keep all three prints distinct, image-only and free of private faces or readable personal documents. Variables: date, location and print subjects.
```
